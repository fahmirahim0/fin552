Lecturer sign in

Use the ADMIN_KEY you set in Cloudflare. It is kept only for this browser tab and is never shown to students.

1 · Your class list

Paste your class list (student ID, name and group, one student per line, from iStudent or Excel) or load a CSV file. Names stay in this browser and are never sent to the server. Only the student IDs are sent, to match them with the coded IDs.

2 · Students

Section A = MCQ quizzes and Quick Drills. Section B = Case Lab questions, Red Flag Hunts and dilemmas. Essays = Part B Essay Trainer answers the student self-marked. Mock = best Mock Test Part A score. Averages are first-attempt marks. Click a column title to sort.

3 · By subtopic

Section B average first-attempt mark by subtopic for the students shown above, weakest first. Low averages show where the class needs help.

4 · Common mistakes

How often each type of mistake ("Why was I wrong?") appeared in first attempts, for the students shown above.

5 · Reported questions

Questions that students reported with "Report a problem" (students who save online). Check the question in lecturer mode with its code. Students who do not save online send their report to you directly.

6 · Research file with assessment marks

For the impact study, only after UiTM REC approval and after final marks are released. Load a CSV of marks with the student ID in the first column and one column per assessment (for example ID, Test 1, Test 2, Final). The marks stay in this browser. The file you download has only students with research consent, their participant codes (the same codes as the research export), usage measures, survey answers and the marks you loaded: no names and no student IDs.

6 · Manage

Reset a student's PIN

The student's next sign-in sets a new PIN. Their progress is kept.

Research data export

Only students who ticked the optional research consent. No names and no student IDs: each student has a fixed participant code. Use this only for research approved by the ethics committee.

Remove a student's data (withdrawal)

Deletes the student's account, progress and results on the server for this course. This cannot be undone. Their own device keeps its copy.
