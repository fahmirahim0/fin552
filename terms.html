4. Your data

  • Without an account, your progress and results stay in your own browser.
  • If you save your progress online (student ID and PIN), your levels and results are stored on the PraktiFin server so you can use them on any device and your lecturer can see class progress. Your name is not sent. Your student ID is stored only in coded form. Your PIN is stored only in coded form. Your working paper stays on your device.
  • The server is provided by Cloudflare, which may store data outside Malaysia.
  • Lecturers see the progress of their own class groups only. They match students to names using their own class list, which stays in their browser.
  • Research use is optional. Your results, without your name or student ID, are used in research on teaching and learning only if you tick the research consent. Ticking it or not does not affect your marks, and you can change it at any time in Account. Research use follows the approval of the university's research ethics committee.
  • You can ask your lecturer to reset your PIN, or ask the developer to remove your data from the server.

5. Lecturer keys

A lecturer key is personal. Keep it private and do not share it with students or colleagues. Use the data you see only to support the students you teach. Lecturer keys do not give access to research data. Do not use PraktiFin data in your own research without your own ethics approval and the developer's agreement. A key may be revoked at any time.
