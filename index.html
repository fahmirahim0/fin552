How PraktiFin works

  • Every question is marked on your first check, then the app shows hints and a full worked answer.
  • Adaptive levels: score well and the questions get harder; struggle and they step back.
  • Works in any browser on a laptop, iPad or phone, and can be installed on your home screen. Your progress stays in your own browser, and you can save it to move between devices.
