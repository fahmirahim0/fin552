Lecturer sign in

Use the ADMIN_KEY you set in Cloudflare. It is kept only for this browser tab and is never shown to students.

1 · Your class list

Paste your class list (student ID, name and group, one student per line, from iStudent or Excel) or load a CSV file. Names stay in this browser and are never sent to the server. Only the student IDs are sent, to match them with the coded IDs.

2 · Students

Part 1 = theory sets (MCQ and True/False). Part 2 = calculation and case questions. Average is the first-attempt mark. Click a column title to sort.

3 · By topic

Average first-attempt mark by topic for the students shown above. Low averages show where the class needs help.

4 · Manage

Reset a student's PIN

The student's next sign-in sets a new PIN. Their progress is kept.

Research data export

Only students who ticked the optional research consent. No names and no student IDs: each student has a fixed participant code. Use this only for research approved by the ethics committee.

Remove a student's data (withdrawal)

Deletes the student's account, progress and results on the server for this course. This cannot be undone. Their own device keeps its copy.
